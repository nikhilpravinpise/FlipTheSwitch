# 10. Decision record

Back to the [master plan](README.md). Scores are prospective judgments on a 0-10 scale, not measured outcomes or forecasts of judging. Each option vector is `Switch, technical, creativity, presentation, hours, risk`; higher hours means fewer builder-hours, higher risk means lower delivery or integrity risk. Rubric weights are the published [30/25/25/20](https://www.devtoolsacademy.com/hackathons/flip-the-switch). The common metric, out of 10, is `0.70*(0.30*S + 0.25*T + 0.25*C + 0.20*P) + 0.15*H + 0.15*R`. This retains judging priorities while explicitly valuing solo effort and risk. Values express this plan's assumptions; ties or changes to feasibility trigger the observation below. The chosen native gate remains conditional on runtime certification, not guaranteed by a score.

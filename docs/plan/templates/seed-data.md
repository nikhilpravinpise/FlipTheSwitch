## Protocol

`PRT-007 Crystal violet biofilm assay`, version 3, released. Reagent needs per plate: crystal violet 0.1% 20 mL, acetic acid 30% 20 mL, LB 25 mL. Steps (short): overnight culture, dilute 1:100, add EGCG series (0, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64 ug/mL), static 24 h at 37C, wash, stain 15 min, wash, solubilize, read OD590. Hazards: crystal violet (toxic), acetic acid (corrosive), E. coli K-12 (BSL-1). Required training: plate reader, BSC A.

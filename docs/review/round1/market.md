## Verdict

The academic coordination problem is plausible, but this is not yet an evidence-backed academic-lab business case.
The strongest differentiated demonstration is a corrected dataset invalidating its figure, paragraph and prior release decision in a shared Switch room.
Problem 1: the competitor matrix materially understates current competition, including Scispot, Edison, Cenevo, Calira, Slack and Teams.
Problem 2: PI approval of routine operations creates an unvalidated bottleneck; the plan has no observed target-lab incident or adoption commitment yet.
Problem 3: metadata verification, local order approval and self-hosting are repeatedly promoted into stronger scientific, operational or privacy guarantees than the design supports.
Score: **5.0/10**, using evidence quality 2.2/3, competitive accuracy 1.0/3, adoption/wedge credibility 1.0/2, and measurable impact 0.8/2.
The proposed 21.5/25 creativity-and-impact score is a target, not an assessment supported by results; one point on its ten-point scale changes the total by 2.5 points.
Review checked live sources on 2026-11-04; all 70 unique external URLs in README, 01, 02 and 09 were requested. No confirmed dead URL was found; access failures and unsupported claims are distinguished below.

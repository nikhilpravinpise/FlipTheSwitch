## Verdict

The plan identifies the right critical risk: a real Switch decision must precede a durable, revision-bound mutation.
The full scope is not feasible in the allocated solo time: the original schedule contains about 41 active hours before submission, while the bottom-up estimate below is 71-109 hours.
The approval path has an unproven trust boundary, and the proposed bypass hook can fail open.
The assay treats technical observations as if a warning were enough to support biological inference; the synthetic fixture also contains a wrong well-to-dose mapping.
The test catalogue is broad, but several edge cases cite tests that do not exercise the claimed failure, and its runtime budgets conflict with its own timeouts and repeats.
Score: **4/10**. Rubric: feasibility 2/3, architecture and authorization 1/3, testing and reproducibility 1/2, scientific and operational correctness 0/2. This scores the written plan, not a working implementation.
Priority order: prove the approval boundary, cut to one complete vertical slice, then establish reproducible and scientifically honest outputs.

## Verdict

LabQuorum is a promising application integration, with a defensible demo opportunity, but its research positioning currently outruns its mechanisms.
The first major problem is a false distinction: human-guided scientific agent teams already exist, including Agent Laboratory, Google's co-scientist and the Virtual Lab.
The second is incomplete enforcement: an approval, a valid DOI and a run ID do not establish informed authorization, claim support or numerical correctness.
The third is weak evidence for the distinctive feature: changing one CSV and displaying STALE does not prove that the regenerated conclusion follows changed evidence.
The defensible contribution is the combination of Switch-mediated human decisions, exact research revisions, operational consequences and inspectable recovery after evidence changes.
Score: **6.0/10**, using prior-art accuracy 1.0/2.5, demonstrable differentiation 2.0/2.5, evaluation quality 1.5/2.5 and deadline-feasible research upgrades 1.5/2.5.
This is a research-review score, not a prediction of the hackathon result; the event gives creativity and impact 25% and technical execution another 25%.
The fixes below could justify roughly 7.5-8/10 in this review rubric if demonstrated; neither a new model nor a general agent-security framework is necessary.

Review basis: the master plan, documents 01-09, every plan template, the event briefing and both background plans were inspected. Research was checked live on 2026-11-04, with a publication cutoff of that date. The source register below distinguishes papers, specifications, code and abstract-only access. Findings concern the proposed design, not reproduced failures in an application that has yet to be built. Judging-point effects below are explicit reviewer estimates, not measured outcomes, and must not be added together because they overlap. Test thresholds are proposed acceptance criteria.

```markdown
### Related work and what is new

Scientific agent teams and human guidance are established prior work. Agent Laboratory covers literature, experiments and reports with a co-pilot mode. Google's AI co-scientist combines specialist agents with expert feedback. The Virtual Lab models a research team and human-guided meetings. These precedents mean that neither specialist role names nor keeping a scientist involved is LabQuorum's invention. [Agent Laboratory, 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-emnlp.320/), [AI co-scientist, 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.18864v1), [Virtual Lab, Nature 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09442-9).

Traceable scientific reports also predate this project: Kosmos links report statements to analyses or literature. Citation verification has related benchmarks, including CiteCheck, while ALCE distinguishes citation quality from answer correctness. A resolving DOI is therefore a bibliographic signal, not proof that a scientific claim is supported. [Kosmos, 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.02824v1), [CiteCheck, 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.27700v1), [ALCE, 2023](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.398/).

Our engineering foundations are also established. CaMeL studies control and information-flow boundaries; AgentCap and CapScope study scoped authority checked outside the model. Dependency invalidation is familiar from build systems, and PROV and RO-Crate provide provenance models and packaging conventions. LabQuorum borrows these principles rather than claiming new security or provenance algorithms. [CaMeL, 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.18813v1), [AgentCap, October 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.00729v1), [CapScope, 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.08371v1), [Build Systems a la Carte](https://simon.peytonjones.org/assets/pdfs/build-systems-original.pdf), [PROV-O](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/), [workflow-run RO-Crate](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0309210).

LabQuorum's proposed contribution is an application-level combination: a student and specialist agents prepare a lab action in a Switch room; an eligible human authorizes its exact revision; changed evidence blocks current release and exposes what needs review or recomputation. Switch supplies identity, addressing, collaboration and approval delivery. LabQuorum supplies the lab ledger, evidence dependencies and release invariants. We do not claim to be the first or only system with these components.

We will judge that combination by three concrete obligations: a wrong-person decision has zero binding effects; changed evidence prevents export until the current result receives the required decision; and the exported result can be reproduced from included inputs, code and parameters. We will also test whether interpretations remain stable under harmless data reordering, abstain when evidence is missing and change when evidence reverses. These are local acceptance tests, not scientific-discovery benchmarks or guarantees of general correctness. [Counterfactual evaluation of scientific agents, October 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.04915v1).
```

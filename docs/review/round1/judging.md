## Verdict

The approval split is a credible Switch story, but the proposed presentation hides it behind a literature demo.
The three biggest problems are the delayed Switch reveal, a seed state that prevents the advertised booking, and an unbudgeted stale/review ending.
The 3:50 video is feasible as an explicitly edited demonstration; the complete interaction is approximately 6:57-10:51 under the assumptions below, not 3:50 of elapsed work.
The wrong-person refusal is the strongest Switch-specific moment. The stale cascade is the strongest product consequence. Citation checking is useful supporting evidence.
Wispr use is substantially legitimate, but the free-tier limit, Android interaction, Insights timing and privacy descriptions need correction.
My dimension score is **6.1/10**: Switch communication 5, timing 4, factual/evidence discipline 7, Wispr strategy 8, submission readiness 8, weighted 20/25/25/20/10 percent.
My conditional project projection is **68.5/100**, versus the plan's 88.3, with a credible route to **82.75/100** through a smaller, proven, readable workflow.
These are reviewer estimates, not organizer scores or win probabilities. Live sources were checked on 2026-11-04; no installed Switch/Wispr session or authenticated submission form was available for this review.
